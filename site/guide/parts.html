Parts

The surfaces a film is built from, as vector: they zoom without ever turning soft, and each returns the rect its content goes in (parts.js). They are a macOS window, a browser, a terminal, an iPhone, an iPad (lying down or upright, its camera on the long edge), a MacBook, an iMac (on its stand, in any of Apple’s seven colours or the one nearest your accent), keycaps, a switch, a ring, a button and the pointer. The devices keep Apple’s own proportions, and each returns its screen with its rounded corners, so what you draw in it is clipped as the glass clips it.

Your product’s own interface is rebuilt the same way from its source, never pasted as a screenshot: every string on screen is sourced, and no real user data is shown.

Tutorials

The Studio’s Tutorial recipe is a film that is told: one task from start to finish, with a voice and subtitles, as long as its story takes. scripts/narrate.mjs turns the story (a sentence a step) into the voice, the clock and the subtitles. The page reads the clock back (FILM.narrated), so each scene lasts as long as its sentence and a line rewritten is a film retimed; FILM.captions draws the subtitle of the moment.

node voice.mjs --setup chatterbox orpheus kokoro   # once on each Mac: the Python that runs the voice models, and the models
node voice.mjs story.json                      # a voice model ("mlx" in the story): voice-mlx/*.wav, then all that narrate.mjs writes
node narrate.mjs story.json                    # the Mac's voice: voice/*.aiff, narration.wav, narration.js, captions.srt, captions.vtt
node narrate.mjs story.json --from recordings  # yours, or another synthesizer's: one file a step
AUDIO=narration.wav AUDIO_LUFS=-16 PREVIEW=1 node render.mjs video draft.mp4

A story with "mlx": { "model", "voice", "tempo", "level", "seed" } is told by voice.mjs, which says each step, trims, paces and levels it (to −18 dB mean, the mean say has, with a limiter at −1 dB), then calls narrate.mjs; narrate.mjs alone stops on such a story rather than saying it with say. Voices, takes and the one-time setup are on the Sound page.

Measured on macOS 27 with say: the same sentence, voice and pace give the same file to the byte; an eight-step story came to 41.2 s, 33.6 s of them spoken, at about 170 words a minute; a 6.6 s draft with its voice rendered in 12 s.
