Type

Twenty open-licensed families in fifteen sets of display, text and mono faces, or any family installed on your Mac, by its name.

Twenty families live in the kit (template/fonts/, open-licensed, latin and latin-ext), in fifteen sets of a display face, a text face and a mono, each a register of its own. Or use any family installed on the Mac, by its name.

The sets, named for their display face: SF, Geist, Inter Tight, Familjen Grotesk, Space Grotesk, Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Bodoni Moda, Newsreader, Young Serif, Bricolage Grotesque, Syne, Archivo, Unbounded and Big Shoulders. In the Studio, each set is a specimen in its own faces, and the headline is set large on the stage.

Roles

  • display sets every headline, text the sublines and captions, mono a terminal. The product’s UI keeps its own face.
  • The brand’s own faces stay when no set is chosen.
  • A family from the kit is copied into the film’s src/fonts/. A family installed on the Mac renders only where it is installed: keep it out of a kit in a public repository.

In a page

await TYPE.load('Fraunces', faces, 'fonts/')   // before frame 0
// then the family in TYPE.draw
