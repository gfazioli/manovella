Reference

By hand

The quick start without Claude, the files of the engine and the scripts: render, beats, voice, mix and deliver.

Everything Claude does goes through scripts you can run yourself, from the skill’s folder: ~/.claude/plugins/cache/manovella/manovella/<version>/skills/site-promo-film, the newest version. The skills’ SKILL.md files are the method (how to ask, storyboard, source the brand, direct, verify); this page is the tooling.

Quick start

W=~/Downloads/acme-promo-work && mkdir -p $W/src
cp template/{reel.html,film.js,fx.js,parts.js,type.js} scripts/{render.mjs,contact.mjs,deliver.mjs,clickcheck.mjs,layoutcheck.mjs,thermo.swift} $W/src/
cd $W/src && npm i playwright-core
PAGE=reel.html node render.mjs sheet sheet && node contact.mjs sheet sheet.png 4   # stills to review
PAGE=reel.html PREVIEW=1 node render.mjs video ../draft.mp4                       # a draft, ~16x faster
PAGE=reel.html node render.mjs video ../acme-promo.mp4                            # the master
PAGE=reel.html node render.mjs stills stills 0                                    # its cover
node deliver.mjs ~/Downloads/acme-promo ../acme-promo.mp4 stills/t00.00.png=acme-promo-poster.png
DRY=1 node deliver.mjs trash $W --kit <repo>/scripts/film                         # once the film is out

What is in it

FileWhat it is
template/promo.htmlA loop: one object travelling through the product’s surfaces, a real cursor, 3D in WebGL.
template/reel.htmlA long film on a timeline: six scenes, six formats, five transitions, a look per chapter.
template/film.jsThe stage (subpixel jitter), rasters that hold still, springs and cameras, the timeline, transitions, effects.
template/fx.jsThe looks: backdrops, fields, pictures, light, texture, glass, click ripples, palettes, chapter sequences.
template/type.jsHeadlines: thirteen styles of arrival, an emphasis, a pace, TYPE.load for a family’s faces.
template/parts.jsVector parts: a macOS window, a browser, a terminal, an iPhone, an iPad, a MacBook, an iMac, keycaps, a switch, a ring.
template/fonts/Twenty open-licensed families with their licences, and fonts.json.
../release-film/template/release.htmlA “what’s new” film: the change happening in place, on a version card.
studio/The Studio: server, wizard, stage.html, schema.json, studio.json, its self-tests.

The scripts

ScriptWhat it does
render.mjsstills, sheet, looks, loopcheck, video, beats: formats, chapters, resumable chunks, the heat governor, the beat check.
beats.mjsA soundtrack’s tempo, beats, bars and sections, from its samples; the bpm and AUDIO_AT that put the film on its beat.
voice.mjsA story told by a voice model on the Mac instead of say; --setup once on each Mac, --audition to compare takes.
narrate.mjsA tutorial’s story into a voice, a clock and subtitles: narration.wav, narration.js, captions.srt and .vtt.
mix.mjsA voice and a music track into one soundtrack: the music measured under the voice, up between lines.
deliver.mjsThe finished films into a folder of their own, and the work folder to the Trash once its source is safe.
contact.mjsA captioned contact sheet of a folder of stills; --compare A=<dir> B=<dir> [C=<dir>] out.png puts two or three folders side by side, a row per file name they share.
clickcheck.mjsIs every click seen: the camera still, the cursor at rest before the press and through it.
layoutcheck.mjsThe headlines TYPE.draw draws, against the safe area, each other, their size on a phone (9 pt at least) and what surrounds them; exit 1 on a failure, 2 when nothing could be checked (its header lists what it cannot judge).
fonts.mjsAnother family for the kit, from Google Fonts with its licence; check, list.
thermo.swiftThe chip’s temperature without sudo, for the governor.

Sound, from the command line

node beats.mjs track.mp3                                       # the track's tempo, bars and sections
node voice.mjs --setup chatterbox orpheus kokoro               # once on each Mac
node voice.mjs story.json                                      # a voice model tells the story
node mix.mjs --voice narration.wav --music track.mp3 --out mix.wav
node deliver.mjs ~/Downloads/acme-promo ../acme-promo.mp4      # the finished film, into its own folder

Each script documents its options at the top of its file, and deliver.mjs --selftest and beats.mjs --selftest drive their own checks. studio/selftest.mjs, from a folder with playwright-core, drives the Studio through all ten steps in headless Chrome without opening a visible window or touching your prefs.
