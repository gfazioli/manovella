Formats

The eleven formats a film can take, the first decision of a film and the one to vary, each with what it fits.

The first decision of a film, and the one to vary: three storyboards are three formats, not three pacings of one idea. reel.html runs six of them on a timeline, with the stills render.mjs sheet takes at every scene settled and every transition half done:

FormatFits
One-object morphan app with a strong shape language
Kinetic typea launch, a teaser, a manifesto
Browser walkthrougha web product, a site
Bentoa product with many small features
Terminala CLI, a developer tool
Devices in 3Dcross-platform, “works everywhere”
Zoom-througha story of depth: each scene inside a detail of the last
Data storymonitoring, analytics, a quota
Before / aftera speed-up, a redesign
Tutorialonboarding, a long film
What’s newa release (release films)

In the Studio, pick up to three, or leave it to Claude, which chooses three different ones for the product and unlike its previous films. Hovering a format plays its scenes in your colour and look. Beside the formats, the Device card sets what the product is shown in, whatever the format.

Two templates ship with the engine: promo.html, a loop with one object travelling through the product’s surfaces, a real cursor and 3D in WebGL, and reel.html, a long film on a timeline with six scenes, six formats, five transitions and a look per chapter. A timeline of scenes carries a film of any length, and chapters land in the mp4 and in a list for a YouTube description.
